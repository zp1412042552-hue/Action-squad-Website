Action Squad

Player Support

Action Squad is a standalone, single-player Meta Quest game controlled entirely through optical hand tracking.

Contact support

Email zp1412042552@gmail.com with the subject Action Squad Support.

To help investigate a problem, include the headset model, Horizon OS version, the mission or screen involved, a short description of what happened, and steps that reproduce the issue. Do not send passwords, payment information, or unnecessary personal data.

Hand tracking

  • Enable hand tracking in the Meta Quest system settings.
  • Place the controllers outside the active play area; Touch controllers are not used by Action Squad.
  • Use the game in a well-lit room and keep both hands within the headset camera view.
  • Remove objects that obstruct the hands and clean the headset tracking cameras if tracking is unstable.
  • Follow the in-game gesture training and calibration instructions before starting combat missions.

Offline play

An Internet connection is not required to play Action Squad. The game does not require an Action Squad account or subscription.

Resetting local data

Language, progress, settings, mission information, and gesture calibration are stored locally. Clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the game deletes this data. This action cannot be undone.

VR safety

Use a clear play area, remain aware of your surroundings, follow Meta Quest safety guidance, and stop playing if you experience discomfort.
